# Storyboard v2: "Who pays when your cargo is late?" (9:16 reel)

1080×1920, 30 fps. Voice: Kokoro Heart, script v2 (`SCRIPT.md`). Every move is keyed to a spoken word
(`vo2/timing.json`), so `build/build.py` re-times itself if the voice is re-recorded.

**Two threads keep the viewer oriented:**
1. **The hero box.** One carton of phones, the same drawing everywhere: packed, weighed, waiting on the
   tarmac, put on a scale, sheltered by an umbrella, flown away at the end.
2. **The path.** After "Let me explain", a four-stop route sits at the top of the frame:
   `Ticket → Limit → Clock → Protect`. The current stop fills blue, finished stops get a tick.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | "shipping a **box** of **phones**" | Phones drop into the open hero box; its lid closes on "abroad". |
| 1 | "a hundred **kilos**" | A platform scale slides under it and counts to 100 kg. |
| 1 | "**fifty** thousand dollars … **forty-four** lakh" | A price tag swings on: $50,000, then ≈ ₹44 lakh writes under it. |
| 2 | "The flight is **delayed**" | Cut to the tarmac: warehouse, plane, the box on the apron. A "Delayed" card drops in. |

| 2 | "your **buyer** wants every rupee back" | A small phone pops up: "I want my ₹44 lakh back." |
| 3 | "So **who** pays?" | The box in the middle, Airline on one side and You on the other, a big "?" above. |
| 3 | "Let me **explain**" | Everything slides down; the path draws in at the top. Stop 1 lights. |
| 4 | "an air **waybill**" | An air waybill clips onto the box. |
