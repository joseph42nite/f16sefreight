# Topics for "Cargo, explained"

Good topics: one surprising rule or number, a concrete situation a normal person can picture, and
a hero object to follow. F16s serves freight forwarders across **air, sea and road** (its site's three
services are Focus Air, Focus Sea and Focus Road), so topics can come from any mode or cross them.

## Done

- **01 — Who pays when your cargo is late?** Contract of carriage, NVD, Montreal Convention limit
  (26 SDR/kg), SDR explained, 21 days / 2 years, declared value vs insurance.
- **02 — Declared value or insurance: which costs less?** (`videos/declared-value-vs-insurance`) Valuation
  charge (~0.5% above the free limit), premium, airport-only vs door to door, insurance and delay.

## Promised / next

- **Can you fly a loved one's ashes or body home?** Human remains (HUM) and cremated remains: documents,
  airline acceptance, the container, who is allowed to book it.
- **Why does Hong Kong cost so much?** Why some air cargo lanes and hubs are pricier: demand, capacity,
  e-commerce volume, transshipment.
- **When the forwarder, not the airline, is responsible** — MAWB vs HAWB, the forwarder as contracting carrier (part two of 01).

## Sea

- What a 20 ft or 40 ft container really holds (TEU, FCL vs LCL).
- The bill of lading: the paper that owns your cargo.
- Demurrage and detention: why containers cost money for sitting still.
- Why shipping rates jump overnight (Red Sea, port congestion, blank sailings).
- General average: when a ship's emergency becomes your bill.
- How a reefer keeps fruit cold across an ocean.
- Who pays when goods are damaged at sea (and how sea liability limits differ from air).

## Road

- FTL vs LTL: renting a whole truck or a share of one.
- The e-way bill: why your truck needs one in India.
- How a truck crosses a border (CMR note, customs, transit).
- The last mile: why the final few kilometres cost the most.
- Who pays when goods are damaged on the road.

## Across modes

- Air, sea or road: how to choose (cost vs speed vs risk).
- Courier vs freight forwarder vs carrier: who does what.

## More air ideas

- Chargeable weight: why a big light box is billed like a heavy one (volumetric weight, the 6000 divisor).
- Why you can't just put a phone battery in cargo: lithium batteries and dangerous goods labels.
- Cold chain: how vaccines and pharma fly at 2–8 °C.
- Perishables: how flowers and fish beat the clock.
- What is an air waybill, really? (e-AWB, the 11-digit number, the airline prefix.)
- Why your parcel waited at customs: duties, HS codes, and the paperwork that unlocks it.
- Incoterms in one minute: who pays freight, who carries the risk (FOB vs CIF vs DDP).
- What is a ULD? The metal containers that fit a plane's belly.
- Fuel and security surcharges: why the price isn't just per kilo.
- Live animals by air: what travels, how, and who checks.
- Courier vs freight forwarder vs airline: who does what.

For each: one-line idea, hero object, the number/rule that lands, terms to explain, what to defer to part two,
facts that need a source.
